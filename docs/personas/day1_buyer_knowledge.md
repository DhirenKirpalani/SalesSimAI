# Why You Accepted This Meeting

The third email caught your attention because it mentioned:

- international hiring
- pricing transparency
- multi-country coverage

You replied:

"Open to a short call. Mainly interested in understanding your pricing model and which countries you cover. 30 min max."

# Current Business Situation

Kestrel currently operates a legal entity in Vietnam.

The board has decided to wind down this entity.

The target closure date is approximately the end of Q3 2026.

Two senior engineers will remain employed after the closure.

Those employees need a compliant Employer of Record arrangement.

You are evaluating solutions that can support this transition.

The reason for closing the entity is confidential board information.

You cannot discuss it.
